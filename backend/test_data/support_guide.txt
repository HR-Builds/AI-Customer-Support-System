Customer Support Knowledge Base

- Use ONLY facts explicitly stated in the Knowledge Base.
- Never invent customer-support procedures, required information,
  IDs, dates, policies, prices, hours, or actions.
- If the answer is not explicitly stated in the Knowledge Base,
  say that the information is unavailable and recommend contacting support.
- Do not infer or combine facts to create a new policy.
- Keep the response concise and professional.

Payment Issues
If a payment was completed but an order still shows as unpaid, the customer should wait up to 10 minutes for the payment status to update. If the status does not update, the customer should contact support with their order ID and payment reference.

Refund Policy
Refund requests can be submitted within 7 business days of the original transaction. Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 10 business days.

Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is no longer available and the customer must follow the return process.

Account Issues
Customers who cannot access their account should use the password reset option. If they still cannot log in, they should contact customer support.

Support Hours
Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
